About Us

Telling India's most beautiful stories through the lens

India's Premier Creative Studio

Founded by Ayush Uniyal and Ankit Raturi, TrueView Productions specializes in capturing the grandeur of Indian weddings — from lavish Rajasthani palace ceremonies to intimate South Indian temple rituals. Our team has covered celebrations across 20+ Indian cities including Jaipur, Delhi, Goa, Kerala, and Udaipur.

We also work with India's top corporates and startups, documenting conferences, product launches, and brand stories with cinematic precision. Whether it's the colors of Holi or the lights of Diwali, we bring every Indian celebration to life.
